Adult Team-based Training

The Rose Bowl Aquatics Center Masters Swim Team is a team-based adult training program, supervised by Coach Chad Durieux. Swimmers range from novice competitors to former Olympians, grouped by ability in their lanes. Some train for specific events; most train for health and fitness. The atmosphere is a mix of dedication and fun.

Workouts are typically an hour long. The team trains for three seasons a year and helps swimmers prepare for open water, triathlons and other water sports. Please contact the coach before dropping in.

Collegiate Swimmers Incentive

College swimmers who train with the team on their breaks get 25% off monthly and quarterly passes, access to team discounts and sponsors, and prorated entry and exit around their school schedule. Short breaks (up to 6 days) are free for USMS-registered swimmers who swim with the team in the current year. To qualify: USMS registration, proof of full-time student status (or a current post-graduate program), and the Head Coach's approval.
