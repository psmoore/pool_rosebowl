What's different in this preview

Today on rosebowlaquatics.org

  • Two Google Calendars in agenda mode, one per pool, with entries titled like “Lap Swim - Lanes 6-20” and “Master's Team - 10 lanes SC”.
  • To know what's open, a swimmer reads both lists and works out the lanes.
  • Program pages type their times by hand (Family Rec Swim, Masters, each water fitness class), separate from the calendars.

In this preview

  • One schedule. Each pool drawn lane by lane, plus a week view of both.
  • A “swim now” board that counts the lap lanes open right now in each pool.
  • Every hour on every page is computed from that schedule, so a change at the desk shows up everywhere at once.
  • Closures and early closings (holidays, UCLA football) appear on their own, and clear those hours from every list.
  • Each program page shows only its program, and families can subscribe to a program for change emails.
