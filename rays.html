The Rays Swim Team

The Rays Swim Team gives swimmers with special needs the opportunity to learn a sport and be part of a team. The skills, self-confidence and empowerment gained in sports carry into daily life, helping athletes become healthier, happier and more productive students and individuals.

Practices are structured so each swimmer trains with peers by age and ability, and each practice day swimmers choose a 30- or 60-minute session within the team's practice time. The Rays compete in intra-squad meets through the year, and a parent volunteer group plans social activities. All ages are welcome to try out.
