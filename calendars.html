One schedule instead of two lists

RBAC publishes each pool's schedule as a Google Calendar in agenda mode. Below are the same two pools on Pool Relay, drawn lane by lane, plus a week view of both.

Before: two agenda lists

Entries as they appear on RBAC's calendars (titles only):

General Lap Swim - 10 lanes SC
Master's Team - 10 lanes SC
Swim team- 10 lanes SC
Lap Swim - Lanes 11-13
Private Lessons - Lane 10
Rec Swim - Lanes 1-3
  • To find an open lane, read both lists and add up the lanes.
  • Lap swim in one pool is often several entries at once on different lanes.
  • The same practice sometimes appears twice, under different spellings, and both copies have to be updated.

After: one live schedule

  • Each pool drawn lane by lane, so open water is visible at a glance.
  • A swim-now board counting the lap lanes open right now in each pool.
  • Lap swim hours by day, computed from the bookings rather than typed.
  • Closures and early closings show up on their own, and clear those hours everywhere.
  • Each program page lists only its own times; the filter row's Subscribe sends change emails for one program.
  • Every entry is kept once. Putting both calendars into one schedule merged repeated entries, so a change is made in one place.
