Group Swim Lessons

Swimming lessons are a chance to learn to swim in a safe, controlled setting with trained professionals. Learning to swim reduces the risk of drowning and is a life skill everyone should have. RBAC's mission is to make sure every person is water safe: quality swim lessons save lives.

Classes are grouped first by age, then by skill, matched to each age's development while staying challenging enough to move swimmers up a level.

To register or ask questions, call Swim Lessons Customer Service at (626) 564-0330 ext. 601.
