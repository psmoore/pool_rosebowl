Ready to Dive In

The Rose Bowl Aquatics Center Dive Program offers year-round introductory, intermediate and advanced lessons, plus competitive Novice and Junior Olympic teams. The Rose Bowl Dive Team has ranked among the nation's top teams since the center opened, and most of its high school graduates continue to dive in college. For beginners, all you need is a swimsuit, basic swimming skills and a desire to have fun.

Divers practice at the deep end (18 feet) of the Dive & Rec Pool, on 1-meter and 3-meter springboards and the tower. The team's current age range is 8 to 17. Head Coach: Jean-Luc Miralda.

Programs and Training Groups

  • Learn to Dive Day (ages 6–12): a 1½-hour intro for anyone who can swim one lap comfortably in deep water. $35.
  • Lessons Group (13 & under): no experience needed; mechanics, body control and confidence. $250 a month.
  • Team groups: $275–$475 a month depending on the team, plus $50 a year team registration and booster fees (Orange team and above). USA Diving membership is required.

Off-site tower training is available on select weekends. The Breaking Barriers Dive Grant funds Learn to Dive Days, USA Diving memberships and meet fees for local athletes: about the grant.

FAQs

Is there a minimum age?
No, but divers need basic swim skills: they practice in 18 feet of water and must surface and return to the deck on their own.
What's the difference between beginner, novice and J.O.?
A beginner is learning basic skills. A novice has enough skills to join the team and compete locally. A Junior Olympic diver has moved beyond novice and competes regionally and nationally.
Are you closed in winter?
No. Programs run year-round in heated pools, with two hot tubs by the boards.
How do I get in touch?
Call the front desk at 626-564-0330 x404 or email the dive coach.
