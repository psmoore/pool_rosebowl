How Family Recreational Swim Works

The Family Recreational Swim line forms outside the front doors. Capacity is strict, so waiting in line doesn't guarantee a spot.

  • Wristbands go on sale 15 minutes before each session. Your whole party must be there when sales begin.
  • Everyone in Family Rec Swim buys a wristband. Once capacity is reached, no more are sold.
  • Each child under 8 must be with an adult in the water (two children under 8 means two adults in the water).
  • Rec swim is in the shallow end of the Dive & Rec Pool. Lifeguards go over the pool rules at the start of each session and let you know when it's over.

Pool Rules

  • Each child under 8 must be with an adult in the water.
  • Lifeguards have the final authority on the pool deck; swim only while they're on duty.
  • Standard swimsuits only, no street clothes. Footwear to and from the pool is recommended.
  • No gum, alcohol, smoking or glass in the water or on deck.
  • No diving or jumping, no running on deck, no rough play.
  • No flotation devices or play equipment except in supervised lessons. No hanging on lane lines.
  • Adults or guardians must be present to buy for and supervise minors (17 and under) at all times.
