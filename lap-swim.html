Lap Swim

Lanes are dedicated to lap swimming in the Competition Pool and the Dive & Rec Pool year-round, under lifeguard supervision at all times. One or two swimmers may split a lane; three or more circle swim, counter-clockwise. If you are the third swimmer entering a lane, make sure the other two know before you start.

Lanes are first-come, first-served. No reservations.

RBAC Lap Swim Overview
  • Locker rooms are available for personal belongings; bring your own lock.
  • Aqua walking is allowed in the shallow end of the Dive & Rec Pool.
  • The minimum age for lap swim is 8. Lap swimmers must be able to swim the whole length of the lane and may be asked to take a swim test. Swimmers under 14 need adult supervision.
  • RBAC does not currently offer annual passes.

Swimming Etiquette

  • Entering the water: always feet first into shallow water.
  • Resting: while resting on the wall, stay aware of the swimmers around you.
  • Sharing lanes: please be courteous and responsive if a lifeguard asks you to move lanes.
  • Coughing: into your elbow, never into the gutters.

Pass Information

Buy a monthly or quarterly lap swim pass online or in person, or a day-use pass in person. The therapy pool and water fitness classes need separate passes. Youth passes are for 17 and under; senior passes for 55 and over. Financial assistance: rosebowlaquatics.org/scholarships.

FAQs

Can I shower at the Center?
Please shower before you swim. Deck showers are for a pre-swim rinse; the locker rooms and family changing room have showers for after.
Where do I put my stuff?
Lockers are available for daily use. RBAC recommends bringing only swim equipment onto the deck.
Can I use the spas?
Yes, up to 8 people at a time. Children under 8 need adult supervision. Please limit your time to 15 minutes.
What is the minimum age for lap swimming?
8 years old. Lap swimmers must be able to swim the whole length of the lane and may be asked to take a swim test. Under 14 needs adult supervision.
Can I swim laps in the Therapy Pool?
No. The Therapy Pool needs a separate pass.
