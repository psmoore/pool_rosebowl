Jump Right In – The Water is Great!

Water fitness classes take place in the Dive & Rec Pool (86 degrees) and the Therapy Pool (91 degrees). Water is low impact on weight-bearing joints, increases circulation and strengthens muscles in every direction of movement, so classes suit beginners, weekend warriors and athletes alike.

All water fitness passes except drop-in include unlimited lap swimming; a drop-in includes lap swimming that day. You don't need to know how to swim, but you must be comfortable in the water (tell your instructor if you can't swim). Participants must be able to perform skills independently and be ambulatory, with or without an assistive device.

FAQs

How do I register for a class session?
You need an account, made online or by phone. Register online or call (626) 564-0330 ext. 601. Your spot is reserved once payment is made in full (all major credit cards, cash and checks).
Does the Center offer credits or refunds?
With a doctor's note on letterhead, credits are offered if you can't finish the session. There is a no-refund policy.
The class I wanted is full. Can I be waitlisted?
Yes, but someone has to un-enroll from that class for a spot to open, which is occasional. Check with the office.
How warm and deep is the Dive & Rec Pool?
86 degrees. Shallow area 3'6", deepest 17'. 50 meters by 25 yards.
What should I bring?
Swim attire, towel, sandals (the deck gets hot), hat, cover-up, and sunscreen applied 30 minutes before class.
