Water Polo - All Ages & Levels

Student-athletes at the RBAC are led by a coaching staff that includes current local high school, collegiate, zone team and National Team coaches. Athletes begin with Splashball and Intro to Water Polo, which build water safety, confidence and the key skills of the game. Gold Academy is the next step: endurance, strength, speed and water polo skills. After Gold Academy, athletes are invited to try out for the travel teams, fielded at 10 & under, 12 & under, 14 & under, 16 & under and 18 & under.

The program has placed over 100 players on U.S. National Team rosters, has medaled 14 times at national events and won six national championships. No prior experience is required.

FAQs

Do I have to know how to play before joining?
No. Most athletes come with little or no experience and learn through Intro to Water Polo and Gold Academy.
When can I sign up?
Any time. Practices run year-round, and you're welcome to attend one before joining.
Where are games played?
Mostly between San Clemente, Riverside and Santa Barbara, occasionally San Diego. Junior Olympics alternate between Northern and Southern California.
How do I register?
Register with USA Water Polo, then with RBAC at the front desk (fees are paid monthly). Only fully registered players practice and play.
