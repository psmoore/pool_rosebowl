Swim Teams

Rose Bowl Aquatics is a nationally renowned swim team. Success at the highest level of competition comes from respect, hard work, discipline and dedication, and the team focuses on developing the whole student-athlete in a safe, supportive team environment, in and out of the pool.

Rose Buds is the pre-competitive team: an introduction to practices, team culture and competition for swimmers 6 to 17 who can swim freestyle and backstroke and have been introduced to butterfly and breaststroke. The competitive team is for swimmers 6 and older who swim all four strokes legally with proper turns and dives. Swimmers new to competition start with a tryout of about ten minutes; coaches then suggest a group and review progress at the end of each month.

Group Requirements and Descriptions

Groups run by age and ability across four divisions (Senior, 13-14, 11-12 and 10 & Under), each with Gold, Silver, Bronze and Red levels below the senior groups. Placement is at the coaches' discretion. The full requirements for each group are in the team's group descriptions.

Rose Buds levels
  • Rose 1: swims 25 yards without stopping; water safety, kicking all four strokes, side breathing, body position.
  • Rose 2: 25 yards of freestyle and of backstroke; all four strokes taught through drills.
  • Rose 3: legal stroke and IM turns, and dives using the USA Swimming dive progression.

Swim Team Fees

All registration fees and season rates are paid in full before a swimmer enters the water. Competitive team: a monthly membership fee to RBAC, a semi-annual family commitment to the Booster Club, and annual USA Swimming registration. Rose Buds pricing is by group, with an eight-week fee.
